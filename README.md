## About

> I build AI systems that run, not slides about AI systems. My reinforcement-learning execution agent, ExecAgent, is live and cuts mean implementation shortfall by roughly 35% against TWAP in a custom Gymnasium benchmark. I design retrieval pipelines (pgvector, RAG), graph-backed agent memory (Cognee, FalkorDB) and security tooling for MCP-based agents, and I turn them into deployed products with FastAPI, Next.js and Docker. Outside the code I lead the technical side of GDSC and the AI/ML Club at Chandigarh University, run workshops and hackathon teams, and have a record of one win and two runner-ups. I am a second-year student, so I am still learning the production side of ML, and I am looking for an AI/ML internship where I can ship under real constraints.
